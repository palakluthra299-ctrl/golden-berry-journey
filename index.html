Sea Buckthorn FAQs

What is sea buckthorn?

Sea buckthorn is a bright-orange Himalayan berry that grows in the high-altitude valleys of Ladakh. It is known as a natural source of Omega-7, Vitamin C, Vitamin E, carotenoids and over 190 bioactive compounds.

What are the benefits of sea buckthorn?

Sea buckthorn is traditionally valued for supporting everyday immunity, skin nourishment and energy as part of a balanced diet. Its natural Omega-7 and antioxidant content make it a popular daily wellness berry.

How do I use sea buckthorn juice?

Shake well and dilute 20–30 ml of sea buckthorn pulp or juice in a glass of water, ideally in the morning. You can add honey to taste. Start with a small quantity and make it a part of your daily routine.

What nutrients are in sea buckthorn?

Sea buckthorn naturally contains Omega-3, 6, 7 and 9 fatty acids, Vitamin C, Vitamin E, carotenoids and flavonoids. It is one of the few plant sources of Omega-7.

Where does WellWith sea buckthorn come from?

WellWith sources its sea buckthorn from the high-altitude valleys of Ladakh, where the berry grows at around 11,500 feet. The harsh mountain climate concentrates the berry's natural nutrients.

Can I take sea buckthorn daily?

Yes, sea buckthorn is commonly consumed daily as a juice or supplement. If you are pregnant, nursing, or on medication, please consult your doctor first.
