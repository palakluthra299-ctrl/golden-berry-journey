Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 29 September 2026

We want you to be happy with every order. This policy explains how cancellations, returns and refunds work on wellwith.shop.

1. Order cancellation

  • You may cancel your order before it is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 92660 86554 with your Order ID.
  • Once an order is dispatched, it cannot be cancelled — but our damaged/wrong-item policy below still applies.
  • Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full to the original payment method.

2. Damaged, wrong or defective items

  • If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or defective, report it within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp with your Order ID and clear photos of the product and packaging.
  • After verification, we will arrange a replacement or a full refund — your choice.

3. Non-returnable items

  • For hygiene and food-safety reasons, opened or partially consumed juice bottles, capsules or oils cannot be returned, unless the item itself is defective.
  • Sealed, unopened products in resalable condition may be returned within 7 days of delivery; return shipping is borne by the customer.

4. Refund method and timeline

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account used at checkout).
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of approval. Bank processing may add a few extra days depending on your bank.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable, except when the return is due to our error (wrong or damaged item).

5. How to raise a request

  1. Message us on WhatsApp: +91 92660 86554
  2. Share your Order ID and describe the issue (photos help for damage claims).
  3. We confirm the resolution (replacement or refund) within 24–48 hours.
